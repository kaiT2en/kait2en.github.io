With another controller on hci0, a USB dongle for example, probe returned the new index and the PCI core took it as success without the unregister action. The controller then stayed registered after unbind. hci_bcm4377 now treats only negative returns as errors. Reviewed by Sven Peter.

hci_bcm4377 kept ASPM off on the whole link to the Wi-Fi and Bluetooth chip because of a hardware erratum, which held the MacBookAir9,1 package at PC3. ASPM is now off only while the firmware boots. Acked by Sven Peter.

Thunderbolt Ice Lake runtime power offv1 Submitted 11 Nov 2026

The software connection manager enabled runtime PM only for USB4 routers, so the Ice Lake NHI of the MacBookAir9,1 never suspended. With runtime PM for the Ice Lake router PC10 at idle with the display on rises from 33% to 64%. Needs pcie_ports=compat removed, which the installer does.

x86 / cache Fast CPU bring-up after suspendv1 Submitted 11 Nov 2026

The firmware leaves the secondary CPUs with invalid MTRRs after resume and x86 restored them only once all CPUs were online, so the CPUs ran uncached and a resume could take up to a minute. Each CPU now gets its MTRRs back as it comes online. Based on Chen Yu's fix.

Wi-Fi / brcmfmac Deep sleep on BCM4364 and BCM4377v2 Submitted 11 Nov 2026

The firmware supports inband deep sleep, but brcmfmac left it disabled, so the chip stayed awake and its latency tolerance held the package out of PC10. brcmfmac now follows the deep sleep handshake of the firmware and the package reaches PC10 with Wi-Fi associated, without ignoring the LTR of the Wi-Fi port.

Wi-Fi / brcmfmac Cold resume after S3v2 Submitted 11 Nov 2026

The chip loses its firmware in S3 but its registers can read as running, so brcmfmac took the hot resume path and commands timed out. It now reloads the firmware when platform firmware handled the resume and Wake-on-WLAN is off. The suspend service keeps the drivers loaded, so sleep starts 10 to 15 s sooner on BCM4377. The BCM4377 D3 acknowledgement uses patch 5 of Michael Reeves' BCM4388 series, carried until merged: https://lore.kernel.org/all/20260925-brcm4388-submit-v1-5-b5cbc2cde2fb@gmail.com/

Bluetooth / hci_bcm4377 Cold resume after S3v2 Submitted 11 Nov 2026
